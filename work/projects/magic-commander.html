The project

Magic Commander is a free, unofficial, fan-made Commander tabletop I designed and directed as a browser app. Create a game, share a 6-letter room code, and two to four players pick up the same table later — life, boards and the stack included.

Magic: The Gathering is a trademark of Wizards of the Coast; not affiliated or endorsed.

Play Magic Commander ← All projects

My role

I am the designer and engineer. I set the table rules, the room-code flow, and the async 2–4 player experience, and I directed an AI-assisted team of bots to help me build the web app.

What to notice

The live app is a shared table, not a single-device life counter. One player creates a room, the others join with the 6-letter code, and everyone can leave and come back to the same game.

Ownership & scope

Magic Commander is my original unofficial fan-made app. The key art on this page is original artwork made for this site (AI-assisted). It does not use official card art, logos or branding from Wizards of the Coast, and this project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.
