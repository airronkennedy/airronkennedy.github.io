The project

Factory Gauntlet plans a factory escape around pressure plates and switches activated with thrown objects. The document shows how a level can introduce an interaction, give the player room to practice it and then ask for more deliberate use. It is useful evidence of mechanic sequencing and communicating intended player behavior before production. I include the plan alongside implementation work without pretending a design document is itself a downloadable game.

What to notice

Focus on the teaching sequence: where a mechanic is first encountered, where it is practiced and how a later situation builds on that knowledge.
