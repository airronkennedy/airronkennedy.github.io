The project

V1ct0r is a proposed tank-support character created for a Borderlands 2 character-design exercise. The presentation explores the character’s mechanical identity through proposed abilities and skill trees. Designing for an existing game means working within a recognizable tone and system context rather than inventing every constraint. The PowerPoint and PDF versions make the concept easy to review as both a presentation and a reference document.

What to notice

Look at how the tank-support role is expressed in mechanical choices. Discuss the alternatives a skill tree offers rather than treating a character concept as a list of unrelated abilities.
