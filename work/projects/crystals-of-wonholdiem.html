Game Design · Game Design I

The project

Crystals of Wonholdiem is an all-versus-all strategy card game for two to four players. Players act as a mage’s apprentice, collecting seven elemental crystals while using action cards to advance their own position or disrupt rivals. I preserved both the early design and closing-design rules so the changes are visible. Revisions clarified setup, hand limits and the number of actions allowed per turn, turning the concept into a more teachable ruleset.
