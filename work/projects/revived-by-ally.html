Game Design · Systems Design

The project

I designed a two-player race around dice and tactical action pieces: Block, Space Swap, Double Roll and Trap. The final rules and weekly journal show how those options changed through playtesting, rather than presenting the finished rules without their history. Early effects allowed repeated turn-skipping, so I revised piece effects and limits to keep both players participating. This is a compact example of balancing a competitive system around player agency and pacing.
