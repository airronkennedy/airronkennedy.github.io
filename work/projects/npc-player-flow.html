The project

My applied thesis explores how non-player characters can be designed to induce and enhance player flow. It connects the history and role of NPCs with AI design choices, immersion and the experience of sustained engagement. The portfolio also preserves earlier journal and research-paper versions, making the development of the inquiry visible. This is a sustained academic research story rather than a claim that I deployed a proprietary AI system or measured an industry-wide engagement improvement.
