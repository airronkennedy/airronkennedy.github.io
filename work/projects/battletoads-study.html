The project

Our team investigated how clearly the Battletoads remaster introduces its early mechanics. Ten participants played thirty-minute sessions under consistent controller, difficulty, seating and audio conditions, with pre- and post-session surveys. The report examines onboarding, combos, character selection and collectibles against the team’s hypotheses. It supports a discussion of how apparently clear instructions can still leave mechanics undiscovered or unused.

What to notice

Look at the research questions, controls and limitations before focusing on result percentages. The report’s gap between self-reported clarity and mechanic use is an especially useful usability discussion.

Ownership & scope
