Research & Thesis · Game Usability & Testing

The project

This paper considers cognitive development and its implications for game design, UX and publishing for players aged six to twelve. It treats audience context as part of the design problem instead of assuming every player approaches mechanics in the same way. The work is one part of a three-paper series spanning different age groups. Together, the series shows a habit of questioning the assumptions behind interaction and communication choices.

What to notice

Focus on a design implication and the research reasoning behind it. Use the neighboring age-group papers for comparison rather than reducing players to broad stereotypes.
