Game Design · Systems analysis

The project

I broke Age of Empires III down into the decisions a player makes, charting each core mechanic as a flowchart: collecting resources, exploring, building, attacking and defending, plus a feedback loop for keeping gold flowing and an overall game flow from the main menu to victory while playing as the French.

Each chart starts from a player question (Do you have settlers? Is there an enemy to attack? Do you have enough resources to build?) and branches into the actions the game expects, returning to the main loop when the task is done.

What to notice

The gold feedback loop shows how a resource source, its depletion and the player response connect; the decision charts show how settlers, troops and buildings feed one another in a real-time strategy economy.

Ownership & scope

An analysis of an existing commercial game (Age of Empires III); the flowcharts are my own work. Source: “Age of Empires Mechanics Flowcharts” document in my Website Projects folder.
