The project

Airron’s Fall is an original, browser-playable arena survival roguelite I designed and directed. It has a medieval look inspired by Dark Souls and Destiny. You choose one of 42 keepers and fight to last a 25-minute run, then face the Ash Sovereign.

Weapons evolve when you pair them with a matching passive. Between runs you spend gold in the Banked Tithe, and the field holds pickups, missions and unlock objectives. A Compendium, controller and keyboard support, and a live player feedback chat are part of the game.

I am the solo game designer and director. I set the systems, the tone and the feel of a run, and I directed an AI-assisted team of bots to help me build and ship the game through 109 merged pull requests.

How it plays

  • A 25-minute run. Survive waves of ash creatures on a top-down field, then face the Ash Sovereign when the clock hits 25:00.
  • 42 keepers. Each keeper has a starting weapon and a passive. Unlock more through missions and objectives.
  • Weapon evolutions. A weapon evolves when you pair it with its matching passive.
  • The Banked Tithe. Gold from a run is spent on permanent rites, and the field holds pickups once those rites are bought.
  • Compendium and feedback. The game includes a Compendium of keepers, weapons, foes and laws, plus a live player feedback chat.
  • Controls. Keyboard and standard gamepads are both supported, including menus, confirmations and in-run movement.

How it was built

The playable game is a static web app at airronkennedy.com/arena, written in React and TypeScript, with a hand-written HTML5 Canvas engine. I directed the work in the open, merging 109 pull requests to get the game into players’ browsers.

Ownership & scope

Airron’s Fall is my original game. Key art and the in-game stills on this page come from the game’s own trailer and play sessions. The game is playable now in a web browser; this page does not offer a downloadable executable.
