The project

I examined iterative project management, feedback and teamwork through an Agile-methodology research paper. The work supports production literacy by connecting process ideas to the practical needs of collaborative development. It is paired in the portfolio with capstone shadowing and later tracking artifacts so the topic is not isolated from project evidence. The paper is a communication and analysis sample, not a certification claim.
