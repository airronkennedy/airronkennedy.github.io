Game Design · Game Design

The project

I used a turn-based physical prototype to investigate choices intended for an FPS/MOBA concept. Eight documented playtest rounds explore map perspective, route selection, HP, battle randomness, recovery, Leech Life, rerolls and enemy timing. The observations show both productive changes and new problems: randomness could create frustration, while recovery and rerolls changed the player’s sense of control. The final document compares original and revised rules and reflects on the limits of translating real-time decisions into turn-based play.

What to notice

Follow one complete chain from observation to proposed change to the next test. Recovery and reroll decisions are especially useful examples of balancing challenge against perceived agency.
