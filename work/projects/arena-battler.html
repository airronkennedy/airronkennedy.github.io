The project

Arena Battler is a rogue-like arena FPS prototype built to explore time as a resource. Across a proposed five-round enemy structure, players earn currency and choose between spending it on upgrades or saving it for more time. My individual contribution log records the high concept, prototype pitch, Blueprint scripting, mechanic iterations, research questions, gameplay flowchart and weekly presentations. The team postmortem adds a second perspective on balancing choices and working within production constraints.
