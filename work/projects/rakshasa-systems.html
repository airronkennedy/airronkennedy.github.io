The project

This competency evaluation examines Rakshasa The Call of Hanuman through movement, combat, puzzles, interaction and exploration. I broke the experience into systems that can be discussed separately while still considering their role in the whole game. The report complements original prototypes by showing how I inspect and explain an existing design. It is a written systems-analysis sample rather than a playable submission.
