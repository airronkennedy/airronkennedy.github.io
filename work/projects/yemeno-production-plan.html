Production & QA · Agile planning

The project

An Agile/Scrum production plan for a proposed game, “Yemeno and the Blind Fox”. The workbook contains a prioritized feature list, a master task list with estimated hours and durations, a capacity plan for 10-hour days across April–June sprints (accounting for meetings and holidays), and a backlog-to-sprint plan assigning tasks to team roles.

Features are coded by category (for example GP.C.1.0 “Models for player characters”), each with a priority and difficulty rating, then broken into estimated subtasks such as customizable body shape, dynamic events and NPC interactions, each followed by a test-and-validate task.

What to notice

Look for the chain from feature priority to task estimate to sprint capacity: the plan accounts for meeting hours and holidays before work is assigned, and every feature ends with a validation task.
