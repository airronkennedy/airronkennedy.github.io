The project

This paper considers young-adult identity and related implications for game design. It completes the audience-design series by extending the discussion beyond children and adolescents. The artifact supports a broader view of player motivation and the context in which mechanics and presentation are experienced. It is included as evidence of research-backed design reasoning, not as a promise that a particular audience response has already been measured.
