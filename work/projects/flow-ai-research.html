The project

The later Flow and AI research paper revisits how game AI can support player experience. It belongs to the same sustained inquiry as the Methods/UX journal and the final NPC thesis, but it is preserved as its own submitted version. This makes the research progression inspectable rather than compressing several courses into one undated claim. The paper can support a conversation about sources, reasoning and the translation from research to a design question.
