The project

This capstone shadowing log records exposure to production milestones, timelines and staffing work. It provides a practical companion to the Agile paper by documenting what was observed in a production context. The artifact is useful when explaining how planning concepts relate to the coordination behind development. I present it as shadowing experience and written observation rather than inflating it into ownership of the capstone project.
