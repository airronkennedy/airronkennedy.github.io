Playable Projects · Level design

The project

A Unity level built around a network of brick train tunnels. My project files show the level’s progression from top-down layout and scale passes, through dressed tunnel sections with crates, barrels, carts, water channels and tracks, to a lighting pass and an in-editor walkthrough recorded in August 2022.

  • Unity
  • Level layout
  • Set dressing
  • Lighting

What to notice

Compare the early layouts with the dressed sections: the route keeps its overall shape while props, water and track details are added to guide the player and break up long corridors. The final screenshots show lighting added along the tracks.

Ownership & scope

Screenshots and the walkthrough video come from the “Train” folder of my Level Design work (Aug 2022). Environment assets such as brick, crate and barrel models may be third-party; the layout and level construction are my work. No playable build is offered.
