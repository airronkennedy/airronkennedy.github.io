The project

Utopianaries’ Vision combines first-person action with MOBA-style progression, structures and battlefield control. The team design document covers the high concept, audience, systems, level design, UI wireframes and narrative. It provides a broad view of how a hybrid game can be communicated before every part exists as a finished implementation. My portfolio pairs this document with separate QA development research so a reviewer can connect design intent to features and requirements.

High concept & vision

“Utopianaries’ Vision is all about controlling the battlefield”: high-action FPS mechanics combined with strategic MOBA progression. The mission is a more user-friendly take on the classic MOBA, using the familiar FPS perspective so players already know the fundamentals.

  • Low initial skill floor: players grasp the basics quickly, unlike traditional MOBAs
  • Familiar perspective: keeps FPS mechanics to attract FPS players
  • New objectives: MOBA goals brought into the FPS playstyle
  • Modern-fantasy aesthetic and story

Audience, genre & platform

  • Audience: FPS players (cited as 47% of gamers) and MOBA players; 52% of gamers play on PC
  • Genre: FPS/MOBA hybrid (FPS perspective; MOBA progression, leveling, AI and win/lose conditions)
  • Rating target: T for Teen
  • Platform: PC/Steam, with projected minimum specs (Windows 7, Core 2 Duo, Intel HD 4000, 4 GB RAM)
  • Tools: Unreal, Blender, Audacity, Visual Studio

Gameplay & rules

Players spawn defending a main structure and two sub-structures while pushing the enemy’s; structures must fall in order, jungle camps off the main road give bonus EXP, and players level 1–20 each match, upgrading three abilities. The match ends when a main structure is destroyed.

  • Core actions: movement/jump, abilities, EXP gain, destroying structures, clearing enemy camps, basic attacks
  • Goals: main (destroy the enemy main structure), sub (destroy sub-structures), moment-to-moment (manage followers, defend, clear camps, attack)
  • Rules: timed respawns for players, followers and camps; structures can only be attacked in sequence; three abilities per player
  • Controls: WASD, LMB attack, Q/E/R abilities, 1/2/3 upgrades
  • Design guideline: translate MOBA enemies and verticality to a first-person camera, with an underdog feel

Systems & objects

  • Enemy AI: respawning, attacking, movement, health
  • Leveling: EXP, skill points, upgrades
  • Character: abilities, movement, ammo, reloading, health, respawning
  • Feedback: visual (death, elimination, level-up, victory/defeat, ability UI) and audio cues for each
  • Object breakdown with stats for Followers (AI minions), Utopianaries (players), enemy camps, structures and main structures

Level, UI & narrative

A single modern-fantasy battleground of ruined suburbs reshaped by “Will”, with one road splitting the map vertically, 4 jungle camps and 6 structures (2 main). UI is sleek and minimalist with transparent ability icons; the document includes a menu flow chart, HUD and menu wireframes and a storyboard of the player gaining EXP from a kill. The narrative casts Utopianaries as visionaries competing in a colosseum-inspired arena to impose their Will, with loyal Followers as minions.

Scope: MVP & backlog

The MVP strips the concept to the MOBA progression system paired with FPS mechanics and the core objective of destroying the enemy main structure. Backlog: online servers, developed character abilities and enemy stat spreadsheets.

What to notice
