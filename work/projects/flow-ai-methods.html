Research & Thesis · Methods & the User Experience

The project

This journal connects AI and finite-state-machine concepts with player flow and immersion. It is an early part of the research thread that continues through later papers and the applied thesis. The work shows how implementation choices can be discussed in terms of the experience they support, rather than as technology in isolation. Keeping the version distinct helps a reviewer see how the topic developed across coursework.
