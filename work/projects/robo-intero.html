The project

Robo-Intero is a moral-dilemma game concept inspired by Papers, Please and developed within a five-student team project. The postmortem reflects on collaboration, milestones and communication problems rather than presenting only a success story. Personal tracking workbooks and progress artifacts provide additional production context. Together, these documents support an honest discussion of what the team learned and how process affects the work.
