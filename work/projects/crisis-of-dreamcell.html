The project

Crisis of DreamCell is a cinematic third-person survival horror game from Dark Star Games. After an outbreak of deadly experimental creatures, the player is trapped within DreamCell and must fight their way out of the facility while managing their own condition and supplies.

Genres on Steam: Action, Adventure, Indie. Release date on Steam: To be announced.

My levels

The team design document credits me with Game Design, Level Design and Programming on a five-designer team, and assigns me two of the game’s levels:

  • Containment Gas Leak — after the elevator breaks down, the player climbs floor by floor through a destroyed containment area while a rising gas layer chases them. Each floor has a lift that needs a key or generator, puzzles get slightly harder and enemies increase on the way up, and the player finally seals the room behind them to stop the gas.
  • Escape — the closing dash. The player finds the code that starts the facility’s overload sequence, then races a timer through the ruined city area to the elevator and the last train out, with the facility exploding in the distance.

The document also defines the systems these levels use: the lift and generator progression, keys and locked doors, notes that deliver lore and puzzle hints, limited normal and heavy ammo, and stamina.
