RIFTSTRIKE — turn-based rift brawl (6 Knights vs 2–6 Goblins)
==============================================================

PLAY
  Desktop: unzip, then double-click index.html (works offline from file://).
  Phone:   upload this whole folder (index.html + style.css + game.js + assets/)
           to any static host and open the URL in Safari or Chrome.
           Works in portrait and landscape.

HOW IT PLAYS
  1. Tap FIGHT. Your team is always 6 Knights; the goblin raid is 2–6 Goblins
     (picked fresh each battle and on Retry; smaller raids have tougher goblins).
  2. YOUR TURN: tap your knights in the order you want them to attack.
     Picked knights get a cyan outline and an order number; tap again to remove.
     ALL queues every living knight, CLEAR empties the list.
  3. Press EXECUTE. Only the queued knights attack, one at a time, in your order.
     Each one picks a random living goblin.
  4. ENEMY TURN: every living goblin attacks one at a time, on its own,
     against random knights. Then it's your turn again with an empty order list.
  5. When one side is wiped out you get Victory or Defeat. Retry starts a new raid.

  Every attack fills a unit's gold gauge. A full gauge (glowing ring) makes that
  unit's next attack a 2.5x special. After turn 5 a Rift Surge raises damage each
  turn so battles can't stall.

  Unit patterns:
    Warrior  dash in, 2-hit combo            special: Rift Cleave (hits nearby foes too)
    Archer   arrow from the back line        special: Piercing Volley (3 arrows + pierce)
    Pawn     dash in, axe chop + knockback   special: Hearth Hammer (shockwave)
    Torch    dash in, flame swipe + burn     special: Cinder Sweep (fire burst)
    TNT      thrown dynamite                 special: Big Bang (big blast)
    Barrel   rolls in, powder pop            special: Powder Keg (huge blast)

  Extras: 1x/2x speed, pause, mute. Keyboard: 1-6 queue knights, A = all,
  C = clear, Space/Enter = Fight/Execute/Retry, P = pause, M = mute, S = speed.
