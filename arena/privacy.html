Privacy policy: Airron's Fall

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Airron's Fall is a single-player game made by Airron Kennedy. This policy covers every version of the game.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game doesn't collect, send or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases, and it doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

What stays on your device

Your settings, unlocked keepers, records and run history are saved only on your own device (in the game's local storage). They never leave it unless you choose to export a save file or share a run card yourself. Deleting the game (or clearing the site's data in your browser) erases them, and Settings can reset your preferences at any time.

Network use

The game runs offline. It makes no requests to any outside server. The web version only downloads its own files from the site it's hosted on.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a run card, your device's own share sheet opens and you choose where the image goes. The game doesn't see or keep what you share.

Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted with the game and on its store pages. Questions: contact the developer through the game's store page.
