Privacy policy: Airron's Fall

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Airron's Fall is a single-player game made by Airron Kennedy. This policy covers every version of the game.

What the game collects

Nothing, unless you choose to post on the feedback board. The game doesn't sell any data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases, and it doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Feedback board

The pause menu has a board titled "What do you think? Or any bugs?". If you post there, the name you type (optional, it defaults to Wanderer), your message and the time are stored on a hosted database (Supabase) and shown to everyone who opens the board. Links and HTML are removed and some words are blocked. Don't post personal details. To stop floods, the server keeps a one-way scrambled code of your connection for up to 10 minutes; it can't be read back and isn't linked to your message. The developer can remove any message, and will remove yours if you ask through the board.

What stays on your device

Your settings, unlocked keepers, records and run history are saved only on your own device (in the game's local storage). They never leave it unless you choose to export a save file or share a run card yourself. Deleting the game (or clearing the site's data in your browser) erases them, and Settings can reset your preferences at any time.

Network use

The game runs offline. The only outside server it contacts is the feedback board's database, and only while the board is open. The web version otherwise only downloads its own files from the site it's hosted on.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a run card, your device's own share sheet opens and you choose where the image goes. The game doesn't see or keep what you share.

Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect any data from children under 13. Children should not post on the feedback board.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted with the game and on its store pages. Questions: contact the developer through the game's store page.
