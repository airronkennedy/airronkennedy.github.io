How to install

  1. Tap Download on your gift (Windows PC).
  2. Open the downloaded .exe. Nothing else to install.
  3. If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen) box, click More info → Run anyway. That shows up because these homemade apps aren't code-signed.
